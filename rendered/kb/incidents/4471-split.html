The three problems

  • parser: the shared parser reads every date month first. Every tenant on file writes the day first, so any date with a day of 12 or less comes out flipped: 7 April becomes 4 July. Fixed on 5 October.
  • hint: the booking-v3 form prints MM/DD/YYYY under its date field. People at a day-first tenant type what the hint says, and the form reads it the tenant’s way. The date flips again, through a different path. The parser fix does not touch it. Won’t fix: the form is retired.
  • shift: a date that moved by one day, not a flipped one. The same meeting shows as Tuesday in Lisbon and Monday in Toronto; an invite arrives before its booking. This points at time zones, not at day and month order. Still open.

Why they were merged

Decision 0001 merges on the subject line alone. “Wrong day on the invite” came in for all three problems, so all three landed in #4471. The triage page already runs the cause check; it was not used for this ticket.
